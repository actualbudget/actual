The votes are counted, and the [sidenav design competition](./2026-07-27-design-competition-sidenav.md) has a winner: **congratulations to [matt-fidd](https://github.com/matt-fidd)!** 🎉

<!--truncate-->

## One Winner, Many Great Ideas

Picking a single winner was never going to be easy, and the [voting results](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual/issues/8642) showed just how strong the whole field was. While matt-fidd's design won the vote, we will likely take ideas from every submission as we build. What this competition really gave us is a clear picture of the look and feel this community prefers, and that's exactly what we needed.

## Free Swag for Everyone Who Participated

A huge thank you to everyone who submitted a design. As a token of our appreciation, **everyone who participated is getting free Actual Budget swag.** I've already emailed all participants with the details, so check your inbox!

## What Happens Next

Now the fun part: turning the winning design into reality. Work on the new sidenav will begin shortly.

**Want to help build it?** DM me on [Discord](https://discord.gg/pRYNYr4W5A) and we'll see how we can break up the work.

One honest note for everyone joining in: some ideas will get rejected, and there will be pushback along the way. Please don't be disheartened. That's a normal part of the design process, and there will be iterations until we release. Every round of feedback gets us closer to a sidenav we're all proud of.

Thank you again to everyone who designed, voted, and cheered from the sidelines. This is what a project built by its community looks like.
